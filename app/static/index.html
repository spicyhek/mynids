Intrusion Monitor

This page shows a summarized view of what kind of network activity my homelab cluster has classified, with a running lifetime flow total and recent activity. It is powered by a deep learning model, specifically a multi-layer perceptron, trained on 9 million rows from the CICIDS2018 dataset, with 97% validation accuracy and weighted precision, recall, and F1-score of 0.98 on the training evaluation. Live traffic uses approximated Zeek features; its detection accuracy has not yet been measured.
